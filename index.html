About Me

// Mechanical Engineering student at Memorial University — builder's mindset, machinist's hands.

I am a Mechanical Engineering student at Memorial University with a strong interest in CAD, 3D printing, prototyping, and hands-on design. I enjoy taking ideas from rough sketches to functional models, then improving them until they actually work — preferably before the first test completely fails.

My experience includes SolidWorks, SolidWorks Simulation, Onshape, AutoCAD, Arduino, ESP8266, Python, machining, and technical documentation. Through my co-op and project work, I have designed STEM teaching models, machined components, reverse-engineered mechanical assemblies, and built sensor-based systems.

I like solving practical engineering problems, especially when they involve building, testing, and refining a design. My goal is to keep developing as a mechanical designer by creating simple, reliable, and useful engineering solutions.

Manufacturing

Lathe, mill, GD&T, in-process inspection.

CAD / Design

SolidWorks, part modeling, FEA, thermal analysis, and motion studies.

Embedded

Arduino, ESP8266, signal conditioning, ADC calibration, and Blynk IoT.
